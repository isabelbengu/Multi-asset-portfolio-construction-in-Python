# Multi-asset-portfolio-construction-in-Python

A full portfolio construction case study built around a hypothetical €2M high-net-worth mandate: 15-year horizon, ongoing income need, Italian tax residency. Includes a 2-page Investment Policy Statement defining objectives, constraints, and risk tolerance, plus a Python backtesting engine comparing three allocation strategies: strategic 60/40, mean-variance optimization, and risk parity, over a 15-year historical window with quarterly rebalancing. Evaluates Sharpe ratio, maximum drawdown, turnover, and asset-class performance attribution.
